The problem Layeron removes

Stop running training compliance from scattered files.

The problem is rarely the training itself. It is proving who completed what, when it expires, and where the evidence lives.

×Without Layeron
  • Attendance stored in spreadsheets and folders
  • Certificates created and emailed manually
  • Expiry dates checked only when somebody remembers
  • Hard to confirm whether a certificate is genuine
  • Audit evidence spread across different systems
✓With Layeron
  • Courses, people and sessions connected together
  • Certificates generated from approved templates
  • Validity and expiry visible from the record
  • Certificates verified through a public QR page
  • Important actions retained in an audit history
